About Us

We're a small family operation renting kitchen time at commercial kitchens in the LA area and producing in small batches. Until now, with the exception of a short run at the Crenshaw Farmers Market, our products have had limited availabilty. Mostly on a pre-order basis or for special events.

We're gearing up to make our big push and bring these products to a broader audience. Until then, if you would like to try our products just drop us a message and we will make some for you the next time we are at the kitchen.
