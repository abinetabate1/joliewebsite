Coding is one of my hobbies. I am not super at it, but I am getting better everyday. Recently, I have started digging into AI and LLMs.

My latest build is a service designed for Plaid developers, aimed at improving how spending activities are categorized. Developers can integrate it into their applications to unlock smarter, more personalized categorization. The system combines an OpenAI LLM, Azure Language Service for text classification, and a proprietary database to analyze and label spending events. Users can submit their own categorization patterns, which the AI then learns from. Future transactions are tagged according to the Users' unique logic.
